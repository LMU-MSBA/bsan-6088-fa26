## 5. DCACT scope check

- **D = Define the business problem:** What decision does the revised statement support?
- **C = Collect and prepare data:** What evidence is available, promised, missing, or restricted? Who must verify it?
- **A = Analyze and generate insights:** Where will you stop: descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, or prescriptive? Explain why the problem, evidence, and success criteria justify that stopping point. You may propose deeper analysis conditionally.
- **C = Communicate the results:** What can you responsibly communicate in three weeks? What limitations must accompany it?
- **T = Take action and monitor:** Who would act, and what outcome would they track if the recommendation is implemented?
